About Me — The Baker!

Market day — the booth, the bread, and me.

Hi, I’m Steph.

Before Tortured Baking, I was a corporate project manager who worked too much, with a passion for healthy living. I needed something that would stop me from sending those dreaded after work emails. Sourdough ended up being a hobby that would do that, and so much more!

I built my starter from scratch in 2024. That same starter is in every loaf I sell today!

Baking began as a way to care for my mental health. It gave me somewhere to put my head at the end of a long day, and the name grew out of that.

Then something shifted. I was receiving the same question over and over, “Do you sell your sourdough?!” The hobby I picked up to feel better became the work I actually want to do.

I’ve baked every week since February 2024. Hundreds of loaves. Some beautiful, plenty humbling. Every single one taught me something, and that’s how I got here.

Now I’m home with my two daughters, and they are my most honest critics. Nothing reaches this menu until my family has eaten it and asked for more. If it isn’t good enough for them, you’ll never see it.

Getting to bake for you brings me so much joy. Thank you for being part of it.
